BenchScout is a sports information platform covering the four major North American leagues — MLB, NHL, NBA, and NFL. The front of the site is the live layer: real-time scores, game highlights and recaps, standings, and aggregated news. Behind it sits BenchScout's own historical archive, surfaced through dedicated league sections and an On This Day feature that connects the current slate to everything that came before it.

The web platform is live at [benchscout.com](https://benchscout.com); a native iOS companion, built with SwiftUI and SwiftData, is in development.
