I sit here after work on a Thursday—living 700 miles away from where I grew up, 700 miles away from my parents, 700 miles away from my closest family, 700 miles away from who my best friends were just last year, 700 miles away from my childhood best friends, 700 miles away from my only sibling, 700 miles away from my cousins who are all younger than me—contemplating on life at 20 years old. 

I am curious if I have chosen the right path. Curious of who I was ten years ago. Curious of what minor decisions made a lasting impact on who I am today. Curious if I can even remember the important parts of the last ten years that made me who I am today. Curious if ten year old me—ten years ago—would want to go through the next ten years of life if he knew what they would be like.

I think it is important to preface that although that introduction might make it seem like the last ten years were extraordinarily hard, they really weren't. They were the same ten years of life that everyone goes through from child to young adult to making decisions that affect your life drastically. Sure it can be a "hard" ten years but ultimately those decisions are yours. Everyone has outside pressures but everyone makes their own decisions in the end.

Another preface: this is not a technical article like the rest of my blog generally is so there will be no AI used in writing this. Although I am taking some of the cards its forced into my life like em dashes.

I want to reflect on the past based around a few pillars in my life. Some of those are old and new friends, job and career choices, relationship choices, and opportunity choices. By opportunity choices, I mean things that have needed a yes or no answer that affect many aspects of my life at once such as moving to New York.

Okay, lets get started with 2016.
